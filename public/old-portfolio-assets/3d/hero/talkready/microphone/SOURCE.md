## Visual Assessment

The model clearly represents professional voice recording and is suitable
for TalkReady's speech-practice, pronunciation-assessment, and AI-feedback
concept.

Although it contains more geometry than the other hero assets, it remains
acceptable as one featured desktop object. Its dark materials already fit
the approved direction.

The red illuminated control ring may later be changed to cyan through
React Three Fiber.
